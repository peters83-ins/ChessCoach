# Piece artwork

The board renders the SVG pieces provided by the installed `python-chess`
dependency, using `chess.svg.piece`. No separate artwork is copied into this
repository. The pieces are by Colin M. L. Burnett; python-chess documents their
GFDL/BSD/GPL licensing in its [SVG documentation](https://python-chess.readthedocs.io/en/stable/svg.html).

Stockfish is a separately installed executable, not bundled with Chess Coach.
